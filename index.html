About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Qingdao University of Technology. My research focuses on underwater computer vision, dense image prediction, instance segmentation, object detection, semantic segmentation, and frequency-aware deep learning.

Publications

Frequency-context dynamic convolution and a large-scale benchmark dataset for underwater dense image prediction

Xingfa Wang, Chenggang Dai, Chengjun Chen, Kunhua Liu, Mingxing Lin

Knowledge-Based Systems, 348:116393, 2026.

This paper proposes a frequency-context dynamic convolution for underwater dense image prediction and constructs a large-scale underwater benchmark dataset with 20,000 real-world underwater images, 15 object categories, and pixel-level mask annotations.

HySaM: An improved hybrid SAM and Mask R-CNN for underwater instance segmentation

Xingfa Wang, Chengjun Chen, Chenggang Dai, Kunhua Liu, Mingxing Lin

Journal of Visual Communication and Image Representation, 115:104656, 2026.

This paper proposes a hybrid SAM and Mask R-CNN framework for underwater instance segmentation and introduces the UW10K dataset, containing 13,551 underwater images and 22,968 annotated instances across 15 categories.
