About Me

I am a master's student at Qingdao University of Technology. My research interests include underwater image understanding, dense image prediction, instance segmentation, semantic segmentation, and object detection.

My current work focuses on robust visual perception under complex underwater degradation, including scattering, noise, color distortion, and frequency aliasing. I am particularly interested in frequency-aware learning, adaptive downsampling, and efficient vision models for underwater environments.
